## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original dark-fantasy survival-horror equestrian monument concept sheet for a PS2-style game.
Primary request: create a MUCH more interesting character riding the horse, combining the tragic, mysterious medieval horror of Souls-like games with the unsettling biological corruption and sinister cult imagery of Resident Evil. Invent an original design, never a recognizable character from either franchise. Replace the conventional crowned king entirely.

Character direction — THE HOLLOW MARSHAL: once a revered royal executioner, now immortalized as a diseased saint. A tall, gaunt but anatomically credible rider in an asymmetrical, corroded suit of ceremonial plate over a heavy buckled leather burial coat. His head is slightly bowed toward the viewer. A cracked, elongated funerary mask covers the upper face; one deep-set human eye is visible through a narrow break, with a partly exposed withered jaw below. A chain of small iron reliquaries seals the mask to the collar. A parasitic growth has forced its way out between the right shoulder plate and neck: a compact, jagged fan of curved bone-like ribs, fused metal and petrified tissue, suggesting something alive inside the armor. It creates a distinctive ONE-SIDED silhouette, not a pair of wings and not generic deer antlers. The left shoulder retains beautiful, broken royal armor. A torn, weighty burial mantle hangs from one shoulder over the horse's rump. Large elegant shapes and a few meaningful details; no clutter. His right hand carries a long, notched executioner's sword upright beside the body, point above his head, its guard like a broken ecclesiastical seal. His left hand holds the reins close to the chest. Every grip and attachment must make physical sense.

Horse: a powerful, melancholy warhorse with believable muzzle, jaw, shoulders, hocks, and hooves. A narrow grave-mask barding covers its forehead while leaving its eyes exposed. Heavy ceremonial tack, one broken stirrup, ribbed breast armor and hanging strips of sculpted funeral cloth. Subtle organic corruption around one shoulder echoes its rider without making it an unrecognizable monster. A solemn grounded walking stance with one foreleg slightly advanced, stable and sculptable, not rearing.

Object/material: this is an old equestrian MONUMENT cast as a single weathered bronze sculpture, on a low cracked stone plinth. Human tissue, coat, mantle, armor and parasite are all sculpted in bronze, not actual clothing or living flesh. Dark tobacco bronze, near-black cavities, restrained green-gray verdigris, muted worn brass highlights. Distinguish the forms with broad value groups and carved depth, not bright colors. No glossy yellow toy gold, no polished plastic.

Composition: wide professional hand-painted game production concept sheet. A large full monument three-quarter hero view on the left. On the right, a consistent full side view, a smaller rear view showing the mantle and asymmetric infestation, and a clear close-up of the face/mask/shoulder growth. All views show exactly the same identity, armor, sword, pose and horse. Neutral charcoal-brown paper backdrop, controlled warm grazing light, no environmental spectacle, no labels or text or watermarks.

Style: original Souls-like x biological survival-horror art direction; unsettling, sorrowful and imposing. Painterly early-2000s Japanese horror-game concept art with authentic anatomical structure. Deliberate low-poly-friendly planes, strong silhouette at a distance, baked-in occlusion, economical hand-painted surface wear. Buildable as a roughly 5,000–7,000 triangle PS2 environmental statue with a low-resolution texture atlas. Facial planes, coherent layered armor, sculpted hands and heavy drapery must be well designed, not rounded blobs or primitive mannequin pieces.

Avoid: conventional handsome king, open royal crown, player/pilgrim character, broad-brim hat, plain knight, clean superhero armor, zombies in modern clothes, guns, contemporary tactical gear, recognizable franchise characters, excessive tentacles, giant wings, random ornamental spikes, generic skull helmet, gore spectacle, comic or chibi proportions, smooth plastic CGI, poorly connected limbs, floating weapons, inconsistent multi-view designs.
