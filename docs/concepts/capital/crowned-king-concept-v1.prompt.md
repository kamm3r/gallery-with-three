## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production concept-art sheet for an original equestrian king monument in a PS2-style dark fantasy game.
Primary request: design a visually memorable ancient royal statue for a fading sacred capital, with enough character and clear construction to model it faithfully. This is concept art, not a screenshot or an existing 3D model. Invent a distinctive king who is entirely different from the game's pilgrim player character.

Subject: a stern, aged warrior king on a powerful, anatomically believable horse. The king has a strong asymmetrical face, deep-set eyes, a short forked beard, a low open crown with three dominant leaf-shaped peaks, fitted late-medieval ceremonial plate armor, a prominent weathered sun emblem, and a heavy long mantle draped over the horse's croup. His left hand holds the reins and his right raises a substantial straight sword in a solemn oath. Noble, melancholy, intimidating, not comic or toy-like. The horse has a strong chest, defined jaw and muzzle, alert ears, a braided mane, visible tack and stirrups, and one slightly advanced foreleg; the weight-bearing pose must feel sculpturally stable.

Composition: wide, beautifully arranged professional game art development sheet. One large full monument three-quarter view on the left; smaller consistent side and rear views on the right, plus one readable close-up of the king's face/crown and chest emblem. All views must depict exactly the same design, proportions, equipment, horse and pose. Include a restrained, low worn stone plinth. Plain warm charcoal paper backdrop, ample clean spacing. No writing, labels, logos or watermarks.

Style/medium: hand-painted game production concept art, grounded dark fantasy, early-2000s PS2 art direction. Strong purposeful silhouettes, believable anatomy, economical broad planes, exaggerated large readable shapes, and selective hand-painted texture and baked shadow. Look achievable as a roughly 4,000–6,000 triangle environmental prop with a low-resolution texture atlas. Avoid excessive tiny ornaments or cinematic AAA detail. This should feel authored and specific, not a generic fantasy asset.

Materials and palette: the entire king and horse are ONE aged cast-bronze monument, dark brown bronze in recesses, rubbed muted gold on worn edges, restrained green-gray verdigris streaks, chipped casting seams, deep dark occlusion underneath armor and mantle. The mantle is sculpted bronze, not actual red cloth. Matte hand-painted surfaces, no shiny plastic, no bright yellow toy gold. Warm directional raking light reveals sculptural depth without hiding the anatomy. Preserve readable material separation through values and patina rather than unrelated colors.

Avoid: pilgrim costume, broad-brimmed hat, hooded player appearance, modern clothing, boxy mannequin armor, tube arms, spherical blob anatomy, stick legs, chibi proportions, sterile low-poly geometric toys, shiny CGI product renders, decorative clutter, extra riders, detached or floating hands, inconsistent turnaround views.
