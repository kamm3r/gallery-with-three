Only 2 containers per house (plus 3 outdoors) are live each night, and they glint. Bonus finds: a med kit closes a wound; an energy drink refills stamina.

Speed · m/s

“He wins by never stopping.”

The chase speed (4.3) stays below your walk (4.8). His only burst is the shift: 1 s at 12 m/s, only after 6 s of chasing, with a 30 s cooldown. Sprinting drains 22 stamina/s from 100. Once drained, you can't sprint again until stamina is back to 35.
