Pillars

“The forest is the frame. The paintings are the exhibits.”

1
One body, many genresThe same character, controller and camera everywhere. Worlds retune them; they never replace them.
2
Original and shareableReferences shape silhouette and feel. Nothing is extracted, ripped or imported from commercial games.
3
Finishable and fairEvery challenge can be beaten at walking pace with human reactions, and bots prove it before it ships.
4
A solid worldIf you can see a floor, you can stand on it. Rendering, physics, navigation and the camera read one shared footprint.
5
Runs in a browser tabLow to Ultra presets, no first-use shader stutter, no per-frame allocation on hot paths.

Never break

No extracted commercial dataCheck where a reference's timings, animations and meshes come from before building on them.
The capital is kit-onlyEvery piece goes through the capitalKitLevel.ts builder. New shapes join the /capital-kit catalogue.
Bot-test every mechanicBots get reaction delay, HUD-only information, timing noise and greed. Pursuers stay slower than your walk.
Say how it was verifiedPlayed by hand, simulated, or unit-tested. A draw-call count is not an FPS figure.
Performance changes must be clear wins“Within noise” fails. Keep hot math inline and A/A-check the benchmark.
Pause freezes everything; reduced motion stills itThis covers the clock, platforms, wind, flames, clouds and particles.
Graphics settings never change gameplayEnemies, damage, collision and telegraphs stay the same on Low.
Keep r3f-perfKeep it and its diagnostics toggle, even though nothing imports it.
